From medicine and education to a human-centered method for AI transformation.

LEADAI did not begin with a consulting deck. It began with a leader repeatedly facing the real organizational questions of talent, process, culture, and scale.

A lived path

The method comes from the road taken.

  1. 012006–2013

    Medicine

    Training at NTU Medicine built a discipline for evidence — and revealed a larger systemic problem in unequal educational opportunity.

  2. 022013–2020

    Education

    He chose education over medical practice, moving from learning content and product to the work of building an organization that could last.

  3. 032020–2024

    Scale

    He later led a team building a learning platform serving more than five million users, reinforcing one lesson: change comes from redesigning how an organization works, not installing one tool.

  4. 042024–

    AI transformation

    He distilled hands-on practice into the Ask, Use, Manage, Build / LEAD framework — living the method at work before shaping it into Atlas, Academy, and Advisory.

Put technology back inside human responsibility.

LEADAI is not about chasing the newest tool. It is about helping people ask better questions, create meaningful output, lead hybrid teams, and integrate the right people at scale.
