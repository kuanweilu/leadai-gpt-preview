L · Ask · Advanced (Expert Framework Research)

Multi-AI Strategic Research × NotebookLM Briefing

Uses Gemini for comprehensive strategic research, then NotebookLM to convert deep reports into shareable briefings — so the team catches up with the CEO's thinking the next day, not the next month.

01 · Background

Who
Ray Lu (Organizer, LEADAI · CEO, Junyi Foundation)
Scale
Individual → Team-level diffusion

Strategic-level inquiry needs depth, but if insights live only in the CEO's head, the team can't keep pace with decisions. The old cadence — CEO researches alone → monthly readout → team asks clarifying questions — was too slow. How do you turn deep research into shared, actionable team understanding?

02 · Approach

A three-stage flow: (1) Use Gemini Deep Research for comprehensive long-form analysis of strategic topics (AI in education markets, competitor moves, emerging tech); (2) Feed the report into NotebookLM, which produces an accessible briefing structure plus an Audio Overview for commute listening; (3) Share with the core team alongside the CEO's strategic context — 30 minutes converts "don't know" to "can discuss."

03 · Result

Decision cycles compressed from weeks to days. The old three-step cadence collapses into: CEO researches in a day → NotebookLM converts → entire team is synchronized by the next morning. The team no longer passively receives conclusions; they share the CEO's strategic thinking context.

04 · Level Verdict

This is not "using AI to research" (Basic) or "cross-referencing multiple AIs" (Proficient). It integrates the **expert's framework (CEO's strategic judgment) + AI multimodal capability (deep research + briefing conversion) + the team's real cognitive gap** to create organizational alignment speed. OUTCOME = decision alignment cadence; IMPACT = the team's strategic thinking catches up to the CEO. This is what Ask · Advanced means: integrating the expert's framework.
