Authenticity Is the New Scarcity

In 2026, Sega Cheng published a set of numbers every brand should memorize: human-created content performs roughly seven times better in marketing than AI-generated content, and he projects a US$250 billion "authenticity verification" industry by 2030 — spending whose sole purpose is confirming that a piece of content, a phone call, a transaction actually exists. As AI floods every feed, consumers instinctively skip plastic-feeling information, and authenticity commands a scarcity premium.

This gives Human leads. AI follows. an economic footnote: putting humans at the root is not sentiment — it is a moat. AI can amplify your output, but it cannot live your experience for you. And experience is precisely the signal audiences choose to trust.

The practical implication is blunt: use AI for speed, use reality for anchoring. Publish real photos, real numbers, real names in your case studies; where material is missing, leave it blank rather than generating filler. The share of your content that could only exist because a human was actually there — that is your trust interest rate.

Once AI lets everyone mass-produce content, whatever cannot be mass-produced starts to command a price. That thing is authenticity.
