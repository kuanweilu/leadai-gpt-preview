Frequently asked questions

Do participants need a technical background?+

No. The workshop starts from real work tasks and is designed primarily for non-engineers; an advanced track is arranged when engineers join.

Is the 10–30 size limit firm?+

Yes. Below 10 we recommend AI Dialogue instead; above 30, hands-on quality drops — we split into multiple sessions.

What equipment is needed?+

A laptop per person and working AI accounts (setup checklist provided beforehand); the venue needs projection and stable internet.

Can the content be customized?+

Always. We collect your actual work tasks beforehand as build material — same framework, your scenarios.

How is it priced?+

The proposal reflects duration, headcount, customization, and deliverables; pricing is made explicit after the initial focus conversation.

Confirm whether this is the right starting point.

Complete a short enquiry. We first confirm context and the right engagement model; until pricing is formally published, we will not hide ambiguity inside a generic package.
