Frequently asked questions

Why three to five years?+

Because capability is practiced into existence, not purchased. AI Transformation = AI Adoption + Innovation — adopting is fast; reorganizing how you operate takes time.

What is the minimum engagement?+

The minimum term depends on the agreed strategy, execution, and culture scope and is stated explicitly in the proposal after initial scoping.

Do you work with international organizations?+

Yes. Strategy 1:1s run fully online (Chinese or English); for execution workshops we recommend at least one in-person session per quarter.

How do we measure whether it works?+

Quarterly measurement via the AI Maturity Assessment (score-band movement), alongside your own operating metrics. We do not make unmeasurable promises.

How is it priced?+

A custom proposal reflects organization size, engagement term, and layer coverage, with deliverables and pricing stated explicitly.

Confirm whether this is the right starting point.

Complete a short enquiry. We first confirm context and the right engagement model; until pricing is formally published, we will not hide ambiguity inside a generic package.
